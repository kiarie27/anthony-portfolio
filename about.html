About me

I am a cybersecurity curriculum engineer at Moringa School in Nairobi. My job is to design the courses that turn curious people into capable defenders: writing the lessons, building the labs, and making sure what students learn matches what the industry actually needs.

I believe the best way to learn security is to build and break things yourself, which is why everything I design is hands-on. When I am not writing curriculum, I am usually experimenting with new tools or mentoring students through their first projects.

What I work on

  • Cybersecurity curriculum design and delivery
  • Hands-on security labs and exercises
  • Teaching web fundamentals to high school students
  • Training teams on incident response practices
